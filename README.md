# 🔬 µAmps – Open-Hardware Current-to-Voltage Converter

**µAmps** is an open-hardware current-to-voltage converter designed as a practical, low-cost alternative to the µCurrent GOLD. It allows precise conversion of extremely small currents into a measurable voltage across three optimized ranges, making it ideal for low-power electronics and embedded device development.

With its analog voltage output, µAmps can be easily connected to a multimeter, oscilloscope, or data acquisition system, enabling engineers and makers to debug and verify power consumption down to the nanoamp level.

## 🧾 Technical Specifications

- **Current Ranges:**
  - ±120 mA @ 1 Ω shunt ➜ 1 mV/10 µA  
  - ±1.2 mA @ 100 Ω shunt ➜ 1 mV/0.1 µA  
  - ±12 µA @ 10 kΩ shunt ➜ 1 mV/nA
- **Output Voltage Range:** ±12 V
- **Output Offset Voltage:** <10 µV (@25°C)
- **Bandwidth:** >70 kHz (-3 dB)
- **Accuracy (typical):**
  - ±0.05% on µA and nA ranges  
  - ±1% on mA range
- **Battery:** 2x A23 (±12V)


## 🎯 Perfect For

- Measuring sleep currents of microcontrollers and low-power devices  
- Validating energy-saving or standby modes  
- Debugging ultra-low power electronics  
- Educational and experimental applications

The analog output allows direct measurement with standard lab equipment, providing a simple and effective way to monitor tiny currents in real-time.
